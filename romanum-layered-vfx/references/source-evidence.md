## Observed design characteristics

### Timing

Small cue and travelling curves precede the abrupt first impact. The sustained mass shrinks dramatically before the second release. Energy disappears faster than debris. The clip contains meaningful low/high/low/high/low structure; “charge, burst, fade” alone misses it.

### Shape and silhouette

The primary body reads as a wide ground-rooted dome composed of bands, not a smooth filled ball. Negative-space stripes and opaque dark fragments interrupt the glow. Long curved tapered ribbons connect the compact center to a much larger silhouette. The terminal release includes sharper vertical/jagged shapes and later low crescent sweeps.

### Color/value

Near-white cyan carries the central brightness; pink/violet marks rims and strokes; debris stays much darker. At the strongest bursts, white areas can become locally saturated and broad ribbons obscure parts of the body. A gameplay adaptation should preserve the color roles while controlling prolonged obscuration. Suggested palette values elsewhere are design starting points, not measured source colors.

### Scale and contact

The central body spans several avatar widths and reaches several avatar heights during its peaks. Perspective prevents exact world dimensions. The avatar remains foreground-left and generally outside the brightest core, providing a stable scale reference. Ground rims, lit tiles, airborne blocks, and delayed falling residue make the energy seem anchored to the scene. No target hit, health change, collision, or real terrain destruction is established by the video.

### Layering and camera

Large ribbons cross foreground, banded mass occupies midground, and tiny specks/blocks extend above and around it. Repeated orientation changes across samples support spinning/sweeping motion as a visual description. The framing, avatar location, and tiled horizon remain broadly stable; no explicit camera cut or reliably measurable shake was established. Local glowing halos and small multicolored flecks are visible. Their underlying lighting, bloom, lens artifact, texture, or compositing implementation is unverified.
