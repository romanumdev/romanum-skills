## John Kler: electrical AoE

Source: [John Kler, @johnklerai](https://x.com/johnklerai/status/2107311829915771125). Reviewed October 6, 2026. Source media duration is approximately 5.06 seconds. Actual playback and selected paused samples were inspected; this was not an exhaustive frame-by-frame audit. Seek samples are approximate observation positions, not measured phase boundaries.

### Observed

The effect surrounds a central Roblox avatar on a dark tiled test floor, with white/gray block dummies arranged around it. A small blue-white charge precedes a high-contrast impact frame with dark radial speed lines and dummy silhouettes. A broad bright blue-white ground-lightning ring follows. Dummy parts break or fly outward; dispersed pieces and small sparks remain in the aftermath.

### Creator statement and limits

The caption says camera angle and lighting make a difference and includes a Claude tag. This post shows one finished presentation. It contains no controlled before/after, source code, prompt, parameter breakdown, device measurements, or verified model/tool history. A separate John post's AI claim cannot establish how this clip was made.

### Proposed adaptation

Use charge → sharp impact → ground expansion → aftermath as the presentation contract. Give the charge, impact, ground contact, and fragments separate visual jobs. Keep high-contrast accents brief and compatible with the project's camera ownership and accessibility needs. Use permitted original assets. Cosmetic fragments do not authorize damage or destruction. Compare the result from the normal gameplay camera and in brighter scenes before claiming readability.

## icydxmon: sustained lightning column

Source: [icydxmon, @icedxmon1](https://x.com/icedxmon1/status/2107181530837840122). Reviewed October 6, 2026. Source media duration is approximately 2.69 seconds. Actual playback and paused samples near 0.02, 1.29, and 2.53 seconds were inspected; these are observation positions, not event-boundary measurements.

### Observed

A tall cyan/blue column stands on a tiled floor in a bright outdoor scene. A narrow bright core separates from broader darker blue surrounding forms. Jagged angular side bolts change between frames. Circular dark-blue/cyan swirls establish the base, with a glowing contact area, small sparks, and suspended fragments.

The column is already active in the first reviewed sample and remains active in the last. The clip does not establish windup, impact onset, recovery, a complete cast duration, or a seamless loop. Do not invent those beats as source observations.

### Creator statements and limits

In visible replies, the creator says the effect [is not AI-generated](https://x.com/icedxmon1/status/2107326727164747822) and claims [original authorship](https://x.com/icedxmon1/status/2107279534408270038). These claims were read in the post's conversation; they were not independently verified. A [polish comment](https://x.com/Lavishcryystal/status/2107222771407405195) suggests fading bolt ends. Treat it as a useful review prompt, not evidence of a hidden production method.

No source release, texture rights, code, generating tool, or performance measurements were verified. No sound-design claims are made for either reference.

### Proposed adaptation

Separate a readable main column, angular branches, base contact, and sparse detail. Use connected short Beam sections or approved jagged geometry when those suit the required silhouette; the pixels do not prove either object type. Vary branch motion without losing the calm central axis. Taper/fade branch ends and inspect abrupt cutoffs. Author windup and recovery only when the requested ability needs them, and label them as new design.
