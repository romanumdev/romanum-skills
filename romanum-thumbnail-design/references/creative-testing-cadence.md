# Creative production and measurement

Two practitioner recommendations recorded in September 2026:

- Prepare roughly **nine thumbnails every three days**.
- Aim for **3–6% CTR per thumbnail in Roblox Ads Manager**.

The target's reporting context is Ads Manager at the individual thumbnail/creative level. The original source, audience, placement and sample have not been supplied. The cadence is a production suggestion; the percentage is a practitioner target, not a verified universal benchmark. These notes are separate from the Tizzy transcript attribution in `SKILL.md`.

## Use the cadence flexibly

Plan a supply of fresh creative that the team can sustain. One possible nine-image batch is three different concepts with three variants each; it is an example of organising the work, not a required template. Reuse the canonical character references and retain the concept, prompt and version for each asset.

Producing nine candidates does not mean launching nine simultaneous tests, replacing every live thumbnail after three days, or generating images without an authorised budget. Choose how many to test from available traffic and the platform's supported experiment tools. Carry a successful creative forward while new concepts receive enough comparable exposure to evaluate.

## Attach targets to a defined metric

Apply the suggested 3–6% range only to the per-thumbnail CTR reported in Ads Manager. Record the current metric label and definition, clicks, impressions, placement, audience, dates and sample size. A campaign-wide average can hide differences between creatives; retain the per-thumbnail breakdown where available. Do not transfer this range to Home recommendations, or relabel play-through, qualified play-through or conversion rate as CTR.

Do not mark an asset below the suggested range as a failure by default, or an asset above it as proof of a successful game. Compare against the developer's own comparable baseline and check downstream play and onboarding behaviour; a click alone does not show that the thumbnail attracted the right players.
